title=Portable Characters
description=Your characters are saved on your own PC instead of inside one world, so you can make a character once and take it to any single-player world or multiplayer server.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]A character screen[/b] comes up wherever the game used to start character creation: on a new game, on joining a server where you have no character, and after a death. Your characters are on the left with Create New under the list, the selected character in the middle, its skills, traits and history on the right.
description=[*][b]Pick a saved character[/b] and you skip the profession screen and go straight to the appearance screen, filled in with that character's last settings. It arrives with its skills, experience, traits, known recipes, kill count and injuries, and with what it was carrying if the world allows that.
description=[*][b]Every character has its own name[/b], separate from the name in game, with as many words as you like. Rename and Delete are on the screen.
description=[*][b]Saved about every 5 minutes[/b], when you leave through the menu, and on death.
description=[*][b]Dead characters go to a Dead tab[/b] instead of disappearing. The world's owner decides whether they can be revived. A revived character comes back healthy, with its skills and traits, without its things.
description=[*][b]Injuries travel with the character[/b]: wounds, bandages, splints, and bites with the infection and how far along it is. The screen shows a character's condition before you pick it.
description=[*][b]Keys stay in their world[/b]: keys, key rings and padlocks never leave, and are still on the character when it comes back.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]What the world's owner decides[/h2]
description=Three sandbox options on the page [b]Portable Characters[/b]. In multiplayer the server checks them.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Allow characters from other worlds[/b] (on). Off: everyone starts with a new character here.
description=[*][b]Characters bring their things[/b] (off). On: a character arrives with what it was carrying and wearing, instead of the usual starting things.
description=[*][b]Dead characters can be revived[/b] (on). Off: the dead stay dead in this world.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=The server and every player need the mod. Add the Workshop ID and the Mod ID below to the server's settings.
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42.22 or newer. Tried in single player and on a dedicated server with one player; not yet with several players at once or a controller.
description=[*]Your characters are plain files in Zomboid\Lua\PortableCharacters. Copy the folder to back them up. Somebody who edits those files can give a character anything, so a server that cares about that should switch the first option off.
description=[*]Things from mods the other world doesn't have are left behind.
description=[*]Hunger, thirst and tiredness are not carried.
description=[/list]
